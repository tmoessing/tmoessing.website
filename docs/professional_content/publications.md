### Classifying Ice Skating Jumps Using Machine Learning

company: BYU Student Research Conference
dates: Winter 2024
type: Conference Talk (15 min)
authors: Tyler Moessing, Olivia Harrison, Mary Seeley
advisor: Michael Jones
description: Lead presenter of a 15-minute talk on classifying figure skating jump types from wearable accelerometer and gyroscope sensors on the waist and both ankles (120 samples per second). We built input vectors with a low-pass filter, trained a random forest with tuned tree depth to avoid overfitting, and reached about 80–90% accuracy under k-fold cross-validation. The goal is to give coaches accurate jump type and count statistics so they can manage training load and help prevent injuries. Research completed as a BYU research assistant.
link: https://src.byu.edu/abstract/detail/7896
analytics: byu-src-2024
tags: Research, Machine Learning, Random Forest, Wearable Sensors, Sports Analytics, Classification, Public Speaking
