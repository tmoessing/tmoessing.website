## Ingredients
- 8 eggs
- 1 lb breakfast sausage
- 1/2 bag (16 oz) frozen tater tots
- 1 can (16 oz) jumbo buttermilk biscuits
- 1 avocado, sliced
- Salt and pepper to taste

## Instructions
1. Bake the tater tots and biscuits according to their packages, timing them to finish together.
2. Meanwhile, slice the sausage into patties or crumble it. Cook in a skillet over medium heat until done (160°F). Set aside, leaving a little fat in the pan.
3. Cook the eggs in the same skillet however you like them. Season with salt and pepper.
4. Serve everything with the sliced avocado.
