## Ingredients
- 2 boneless, skinless chicken breasts, cut into 1-inch pieces
- 1/4 cup pesto
- 1/4 cup mozzarella cheese
- Salt and pepper

## Instructions
1. Preheat the oven to 375°F.
2. Spray an 8x8 baking dish with non-stick spray. Spread 1/8 cup pesto over the bottom.
3. Spread the chicken over the pesto, then spread the remaining pesto over the chicken. (Don't re-dip a spoon that's touched raw chicken into the pesto.)
4. Cover with foil and bake 25–30 minutes.
5. Remove the foil, sprinkle the cheese on top, and bake uncovered 5 more minutes until melted.

## Tips
- Serve with rice, quinoa, or wide noodles, and spoon the pesto from the dish over the top.
- For a dairy-free portion, pull some chicken out before adding the cheese.
