## Ingredients
- 1/2 cup warm water
- 2 tbsp yeast
- 6 cups flour
- 3 tbsp sugar
- 1 tbsp salt
- 1/3 cup oil
- 2 cups hot water

## Instructions
1. Dissolve the yeast in the warm water.
2. Combine the remaining ingredients using **3 cups of the flour** and beat well.
3. Add the yeast and beat well.
4. Add the remaining flour to make a soft dough that can still be mixed with a spoon (it will be sticky).
5. Leave the spoon in and let the dough rest 10 minutes. Stir it down every 10 minutes, 5 times total.
6. Divide in two. Roll each up like a jelly roll, or make 2 dozen rolls. Place in greased pans.
7. Let rise until doubled, about 30 minutes.
8. Bake at 400°F for 30 minutes.
