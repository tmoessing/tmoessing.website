## Ingredients
- 4 slices bread
- 1/2 cup milk
- 2 eggs, beaten
- 1/4 tsp pepper
- 2 tbsp minced onion
- 2 tsp baking powder
- 1/4 tsp Accent (MSG)
- 1 1/2 lb ground beef
- 1 can cream of mushroom soup
- 1 can cream of chicken soup
- 1 soup can of milk

## Instructions
1. Soak the bread for 10 minutes in the milk and egg mixture.
2. Combine with all the other ingredients except the soups and the soup can of milk.
3. Shape into 25 meatballs. Brown them in a little fat and place in a baking dish.
4. Mix the undiluted soups with the can of milk and pour over the meatballs.
5. Bake at 325°F for 1 hour.

## Notes

## Tips
- Serve with rice, baked potatoes, or a potato casserole.
