## Ingredients
- 1 quart chicken or vegetable broth
- ¼–½ cup soy sauce
- 1 package Costco frozen pre-cooked wontons
- Baby bok choy
- Green onion

## Instructions
1. Bring the broth to a boil and add the soy sauce.
2. Add the wontons and heat for 1–2 minutes.
3. Add the bok choy and green onion. Cover until the bok choy cooks down.
4. Season to taste.

## Notes
From "Cook Like Kathleen," Grandma Kathleen Moessing's family cookbook.
