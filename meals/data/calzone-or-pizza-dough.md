## Ingredients
- 1/2 cup lukewarm water
- 1 package (0.25 oz) active dry yeast
- 1/2 cup water
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 2 tbsp honey
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 3 cups all-purpose flour, plus more for dusting
- Your favorite fillings or toppings

## Instructions
1. Mix the 1/2 cup lukewarm water and the yeast in a small bowl. Let stand until foamy, about 5 minutes.
2. In a large bowl, combine the other 1/2 cup water, olive oil, honey, and salt. Add 1 cup flour and mix well.
3. Mix in the yeast mixture, then add the remaining flour 1 cup at a time until the dough is well combined.
4. Knead on a floured surface until soft, smooth, and elastic, about 8 minutes.
5. Roll into 4 calzones or 1 large pizza.
6. Preheat the oven to 400°F. Fill or top the dough, then bake until the crust is lightly browned, 15–20 minutes.

## Tips
- Brush the crust with olive oil and sprinkle with garlic powder.
