## Ingredients
- 1 lb Italian sausage (mild or spicy)
- 1/8 onion, chopped
- 1/2 tsp dried oregano
- 1/2 tsp dried basil
- 1 tbsp dried parsley
- 2 cloves garlic (or 2 tsp garlic salt or garlic powder)
- 1/2 cup carrots, sliced and cooked (unless you're simmering a long time, then they'll cook in the soup)
- 1 zucchini, grated
- 1 can (14–15 oz) diced tomatoes (petite diced preferred, regular is fine)
- 1 small can (8 oz) tomato sauce
- 1/2 cup apple juice
- 1/2 cup water
- 4 cans (14–15 oz each, about 60 oz total) chicken broth
- 1 lb tortellini (frozen, fresh, or dried)

## Instructions
1. Brown the sausage in a large soup pot with the onion and spices.
2. While it browns, slice and cook the carrots and grate the zucchini.
3. Once the sausage is browned, add everything else to the pot and heat through.
4. Serve, or keep it on a simmer (or in a crock pot) for hours. If simmering a while, make sure the carrots and any dried tortellini are soft before serving.

## Notes
With frozen or fresh tortellini and pre-cooked carrots, this soup is done in about 20 minutes.

## Tips
- Using dried tortellini? Let it simmer a long time, or cook it before adding.

## Variations
- Add, swap, or leave out veggies however you like.
- Add extra water or broth if you want it brothier.
