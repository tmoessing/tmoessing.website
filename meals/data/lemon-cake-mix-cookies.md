## Ingredients
- 1 box (15.25 oz) lemon cake mix
- 1/2 cup vegetable oil
- 2 large eggs
- 1 lemon, zested and juiced
- 1/4 cup powdered sugar

## Instructions
1. Preheat the oven to 350°F. Line a baking sheet with parchment paper and set aside.
2. Combine the cake mix, oil, and eggs in a large bowl. Add **1 tablespoon fresh lemon juice** and **1 tablespoon lemon zest**.
3. Stir together just until combined. If you have time, refrigerate the dough for 15–20 minutes (it's sticky, and chilling makes it easier to work with).
4. Use a medium scoop or spoon to portion the dough, roll each ball in the powdered sugar, and place on the prepared baking sheet.
5. Bake 9–11 minutes, until the cookies are set. You don't want them to brown.
6. Cool on the pan for a couple of minutes, then transfer to a cooling rack.

## Notes
Bursting with fresh lemon flavor, and they look great in a holiday cookie box.

## Tips
- **To freeze:** Let the cookies cool, then freeze on a cookie sheet for 30 minutes. Once hard, stack in a freezer container or zip-top bag for up to 3 months. Take them out of the freezer 2 hours before serving.
