## Ingredients
**Crust**
- 2 ½ cups flour
- 1 cup butter
- ½ cup sugar
- ½ tsp salt

**Filling**
- 4 eggs
- 1 ½ cups sugar
- 3 tbsp melted butter
- 1 ½ tsp vanilla
- 3 cups chopped pecans

## Instructions
1. Heat the oven to 350°F.
2. Mix the crust ingredients and press into the bottom of a 9x13 pan sprayed with Pam. Bake 20–23 minutes, until brown.
3. Beat the eggs, sugar, melted butter, and vanilla until well blended. Stir in the pecans.
4. Pour over the hot crust. Bake at 350°F for 25 minutes, until firm around the edge and slightly firm in the center.

## Notes
From "Cook Like Kathleen," Grandma Kathleen Moessing's family cookbook.
