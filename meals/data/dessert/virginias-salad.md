## Ingredients
- 1 package orange Jell-O
- 1 cup cold water
- 1 cup crushed pineapple
- ¼ cup sugar
- 1 cup fruit cocktail
- 1 cup whipped cream
- ½ cup shredded mild cheddar cheese

## Instructions
1. Heat the pineapple, sugar, and fruit cocktail to a boil.
2. Mix with the orange Jell-O, then stir in the cold water.
3. Let set until it quivers, then add the whipped cream and cheese.
4. Chill until firm.

## Notes
From "Cook Like Kathleen," Grandma Kathleen Moessing's family cookbook.
