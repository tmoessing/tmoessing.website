## Ingredients
- ¾ cup shortening
- 1 egg
- 1 cup sugar
- ¼ cup molasses
- 2 cups flour
- 1 tsp salt
- 2 tsp baking soda
- 1 tsp ginger
- 1 tsp cloves
- 1 tsp cinnamon

## Instructions
1. Heat the oven to 350°F.
2. Cream the shortening and sugar, then beat in the egg and molasses.
3. Stir in the flour, salt, baking soda, ginger, cloves, and cinnamon.
4. Roll into balls and roll in sugar. Bake for 10 minutes.

## Notes
The original card says only "Roll into balls in sugar. Bake 350 F 10 min." The mixing steps are the standard method.
