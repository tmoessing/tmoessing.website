## Ingredients
- 3 cups mashed peaches
- 3 cups sugar
- Juice from 2 ½ lemons (6 ¼ tbsp)
- Juice from 1 orange
- 1 pint whipping cream
- 1 quart milk
- 1 large can condensed milk

## Instructions
1. Combine the peaches, sugar, lemon juice, and orange juice. Let stand at room temperature for 2–3 hours.
2. Pour into a 4-quart ice cream freezer and add the whipping cream, canned milk, and milk.
3. Freeze!

## Notes
From "Cook Like Kathleen," Grandma Kathleen Moessing's family cookbook.
