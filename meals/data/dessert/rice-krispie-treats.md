## Ingredients
- 3 tbsp butter
- 1 package (10 oz, about 40) Jet-Puffed marshmallows, or 5 ½ cups mini marshmallows
- 6 cups Kellogg's Rice Krispies cereal

## Instructions
1. In a large saucepan, melt the butter over low heat. Add the marshmallows and stir until completely melted. Remove from the heat.
2. Add the cereal and stir until well coated.
3. Using a buttered spatula or wax paper, press the mixture evenly into a 13x9x2-inch pan coated with cooking spray.
4. Cool, then cut into 2-inch squares. Best served the same day.

## Notes
From "Cook Like Kathleen," Grandma Kathleen Moessing's family cookbook.
