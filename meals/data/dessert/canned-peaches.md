## Ingredients
- Fresh peaches
- ¾ cup sugar per batch of syrup
- 1 ½ cups hot water per batch of syrup

## Instructions
1. Cut the peaches in half and peel the skins with a knife.
2. Put them in the bottle with the round side of the peach up and the hollow toward the bottom of the jar.
3. Dissolve the sugar in the hot water and pour it over the peaches.
4. Make sure the rim of each bottle is clean, then put on the lid and ring and set it on the rack in the canner.
5. Fill the canner with water to just below the jar rings (don't cover the bottles).
6. Bring to a boil on high, then turn to medium and boil 20 minutes.
7. Turn off the heat, take the lid off the canner, and tighten the rings.
8. Set the bottles out and say a prayer that they seal.

## Notes
These are Grandma's own instructions. For current tested canning times, check the USDA or your local extension office.

From "Cook Like Kathleen," Grandma Kathleen Moessing's family cookbook.
