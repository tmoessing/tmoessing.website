## Ingredients
- 2 sticks butter, softened to room temperature
- 1 cup dark brown sugar
- 3/4 cup granulated sugar
- 2 eggs
- 1 tsp vanilla
- 3 cups flour
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 2 cups chocolate chips

## Instructions
1. Preheat the oven to 300°F.
2. With a mixer, beat the butter, dark brown sugar, and granulated sugar until well mixed.
3. Mix in the eggs and vanilla.
4. Add the flour, baking soda, and salt and mix.
5. Stir in the chocolate chips.
6. Bake about 15 minutes, until the cookies are light brown.
7. Let them sit on the cookie sheet a few extra minutes before moving them to a cooling rack.

## Tips
- Cookies turn out better if each batch goes onto a somewhat cool cookie sheet.
