## Ingredients
- 1 cup granulated sugar
- 2 tbsp lemon zest (zest of 2 lemons)
- 6 egg yolks
- ½ cup lemon juice minus 1 tbsp (100 ml)
- ½ cup unsalted butter, cold and cubed

## Instructions
1. Strain the egg yolks into a pot and beat lightly with a wire whisk.
2. Whisk in the sugar and zest and mix well until lighter in color.
3. Stir in the lemon juice gradually.
4. Heat on low, stirring constantly with the whisk, until the mixture thickens, just starts to bubble, and coats the back of a wooden spoon.
5. Remove from the heat, add the cold butter, and mix until melted.

## Notes
From "Cook Like Kathleen," Grandma Kathleen Moessing's family cookbook.
