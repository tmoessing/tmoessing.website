## Ingredients
**Fruit**
- 1 quart fruit, with its juice (bottled, or fresh; see step 1)

**Topping**
- 1 ½ cups flour
- 1 ½ tsp baking powder
- ½ cup milk
- 5 tbsp shortening
- 1 tsp sugar
- ¼ tsp salt

## Instructions
1. If using fresh fruit, cook it in a little water with some sugar first: about 1 cup water to 3 cups fruit, brought to a boil.
2. Layer the fruit and juice in the bottom of a greased 8x8 pan.
3. Mix the flour, baking powder, sugar, and salt. Cut in the shortening with a knife or your fingers, then stir in the milk to make a soft dough.
4. Spoon the cobbler mixture over the fruit.
5. Bake for 30 minutes at 350°F.

## Notes
From "Cook Like Kathleen," Grandma Kathleen Moessing's family cookbook.
