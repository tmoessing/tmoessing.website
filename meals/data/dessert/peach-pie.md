## Ingredients
- 2 pie crust disks (1 for the bottom, 1 for the lattice top)
- ⅓ cup all-purpose flour (or 3 tbsp cornstarch), plus more to dust
- 2 ½ lbs ripe peaches (7–8 peaches, or 6 ½ cups sliced)
- 1 ½ tbsp fresh lemon juice
- 1 tsp pure vanilla extract
- ⅔ cup granulated sugar
- ¼ tsp ground nutmeg (or cinnamon)
- ⅛ tsp fine sea salt
- 1 large egg plus 1 tbsp water, for egg wash
- 1 tsp coarse turbinado or granulated sugar, for finishing

## Instructions
1. Preheat the oven to 425°F with racks in the middle and bottom third. Line the bottom rack with foil to catch drips. Fill a large bowl with ice water.
2. Blanch the peaches: add them to a pot of boiling water for 1 minute to loosen the skins, then move to the ice bath with a slotted spoon.
3. When cool, halve the peaches, remove the pits, and slide off the skins. Cut into ½-inch slices and put in a large bowl.
4. Toss with the lemon juice and vanilla, then add the sugar, flour, nutmeg, and salt and gently fold until no dry flour remains.
5. Pour the filling into the chilled bottom crust. Top with a lattice crust, fold the excess dough under, and flute the edges.
6. Beat the egg and water with a fork, brush it all over the crust, and sprinkle with the coarse sugar.
7. Bake at 425°F for 20 minutes, then reduce to 375°F for 35–45 minutes more.
8. Cool at least 2–4 hours before cutting so the filling can thicken.
