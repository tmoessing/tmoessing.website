## Ingredients
- 1 cube margarine
- 1 tsp vanilla
- 30 large marshmallows (or 3 cups mini marshmallows)
- 1 ½ tsp green food coloring
- 4 ½ cups corn flakes
- Red hot candies, for berries

## Instructions
1. Melt the margarine and marshmallows over low heat, stirring until smooth.
2. Stir in the vanilla and green food coloring, then the corn flakes, until coated.
3. Shape into wreaths on wax paper and add red hots for berries.

## Notes
The original lists the ingredients and the shaping step; melting and mixing follow the usual method.
