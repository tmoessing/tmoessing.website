## Ingredients
- ½ cup sugar
- 1 cup dark corn syrup
- ¼ tsp salt
- 1 tbsp all-purpose flour
- 2 eggs
- 1 tbsp butter or margarine
- 2 oz (2 squares) unsweetened chocolate
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 ¼ cups pecan halves
- Pastry for a 1-crust 9-inch pie, unbaked
- Vanilla ice cream, for serving

## Instructions
1. Preheat the oven to 300°F.
2. Beat together the sugar, syrup, salt, flour, and eggs.
3. Melt the butter and chocolate together in the microwave. Add it, the vanilla, and the pecans to the first mixture.
4. Pour into the pastry-lined pie pan.
5. Bake 50–60 minutes, until the filling is just set. Cool.
6. Serve with ice cream, or whipped cream if you prefer.

## Tips
- This pie can be baked a day ahead.
