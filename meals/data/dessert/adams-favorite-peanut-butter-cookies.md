## Ingredients
- 1 batch [Irresistible Peanut Butter Cookies](#irresistible-peanut-butter-cookies) dough
- Sugar, for rolling
- Hershey's Kisses (about 36)

## Instructions
1. Make a batch of Irresistible Peanut Butter Cookie dough.
2. Roll the dough into balls and roll them in sugar.
3. Bake as directed. When they come out of the oven, press a Hershey's Kiss into the center of each cookie.

## Notes
From "Cook Like Kathleen," Grandma Kathleen Moessing's family cookbook.
