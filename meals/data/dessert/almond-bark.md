## Ingredients
- Almonds
- Baker's white chocolate, melted

## Instructions
1. Spread the almonds over a jelly roll pan.
2. Pour the melted white chocolate over them.
3. Once cool, break into "bark."

## Notes
From "Cook Like Kathleen," Grandma Kathleen Moessing's family cookbook.
