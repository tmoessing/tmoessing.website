## Ingredients
- 3 ¼ cups flour
- 1 cup sugar
- 1 egg
- 1 tsp vanilla
- ½ tsp salt
- ½ cup butter
- ½ cup sour cream

## Instructions
1. Heat the oven to 400°F.
2. Beat the sugar, egg, vanilla, butter, and sour cream for 2 minutes.
3. Add the dry ingredients.
4. Roll out ¼ inch thick and cut into shapes.
5. Bake on a buttered cookie sheet 5–6 minutes.

## Notes
From "Cook Like Kathleen," Grandma Kathleen Moessing's family cookbook.
