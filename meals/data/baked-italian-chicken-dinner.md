## Ingredients
- 1 lb skinless, boneless chicken breast, cut into cubes
- 1 package (10 oz) frozen broccoli
- 4 potatoes, diced
- 1/4 cup butter, melted
- 1 package (0.7 oz) Italian dressing mix

## Instructions
1. Preheat the oven to 350°F. Spray a 13x9-inch baking dish with cooking spray.
2. Layer the chicken, broccoli, and potatoes evenly in the dish, in that order.
3. Drizzle the melted butter over the top and sprinkle with the Italian dressing mix.
4. Bake until the chicken is cooked through and the potatoes are tender, 45–60 minutes.

## Variations
- Try adding carrots.
