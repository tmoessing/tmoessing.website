## Ingredients
- 4 cups brown sugar
- 2 cups butter, softened
- 1 cup buttermilk
- 2 tsp vanilla
- 4 cups flour
- 2 tsp salt
- 2 tsp baking soda
- 4 heaping tsp cinnamon
- 8 cups old-fashioned oats

## Instructions
1. Preheat the oven to 375°F.
2. Cream the butter and brown sugar together until smooth, then mix in the buttermilk and vanilla.
3. In a separate bowl, whisk the flour, salt, baking soda, and cinnamon. Add to the wet mixture and stir until combined.
4. Stir in the oats.
5. Drop rounded spoonfuls onto a baking sheet and bake for 10 minutes.

## Notes
Shared by a friend from my mission.

The original family recipe card lists only the ingredients and "Bake at 375 for 10 minutes." The mixing steps above are the standard method for this kind of cookie.

## Tips
- This makes a *lot* of cookies. Halve everything for a normal-sized batch.
