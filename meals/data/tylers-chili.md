## Ingredients
- 1 lb pork sausage
- 1 can (15.5 oz) chili beans, undrained
- 1 can (14.5 oz) diced tomatoes, undrained
- 1 bag (12 oz) frozen mixed vegetables
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 1 box (8.5 oz) honey corn muffin mix, plus the egg and milk called for on the box

## Instructions
1. Make the corn bread according to the box and put it in the oven.
2. Brown the sausage in a large pot over medium-high heat, breaking it up, until cooked through. Drain the fat.
3. Stir in the chili beans, diced tomatoes, and frozen vegetables.
4. Bring to a boil, then reduce the heat and simmer 10–15 minutes, until the vegetables are tender. Season with salt and pepper.
5. Serve with the corn bread.
