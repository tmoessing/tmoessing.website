## Ingredients
**Rolls**
- ½ cup butter, melted
- ½ cup sugar
- 1 orange peel, grated
- 18 frozen dinner rolls

**Orange Glaze**
- 1 cup powdered sugar
- 2 tbsp butter, softened
- 2 tbsp orange juice

## Instructions
1. Combine the melted butter, sugar, and grated orange peel in a bowl.
2. Dip the rolls in the mixture and place in muffin tins. Let rise for 3 hours.
3. Bake at 350°F for 10–15 minutes, or until lightly browned.
4. Mix the glaze ingredients and frost the rolls while still warm.

## Notes
From "Cook Like Kathleen," Grandma Kathleen Moessing's family cookbook.
