## Ingredients
**French toast**
- ½ cup butter
- 1 cup packed brown sugar
- 2 tbsp corn syrup
- 1 loaf Texas toast bread (12 extra-thick slices)
- 5 eggs
- 1 ½ cups half-and-half
- ¼ tsp salt
- 1 tsp vanilla

**Syrup Butter**
- 1 ½ cups butter
- 3 cups maple syrup

## Instructions
1. In a small saucepan, melt the butter, brown sugar, and corn syrup over medium heat until smooth, stirring occasionally.
2. Pour onto a large greased jelly roll pan (18x13x1) and spread to cover.
3. Lay the 12 slices of Texas toast in a single layer over it.
4. Mix the eggs, half-and-half, salt, and vanilla and spoon over each slice.
5. Cover and refrigerate overnight.
6. Bake uncovered at 350°F for about 30 minutes. Remove from the pan and serve. If not serving right away, turn each slice over so it won't stick to the pan.
7. **Syrup butter:** melt the butter and maple syrup together and serve over the toast.
