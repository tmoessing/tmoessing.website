## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp butter
- 1 ¾ cups sliced mushrooms (canned button or fresh)
- 1 package (12 oz) frozen spinach soufflé (creamed spinach), thawed
- ½ lb sweet Italian sausage (Jimmy Dean mild), casings removed, cooked and crumbled
- ¾ cup shredded Swiss cheese
- 2 eggs, beaten to blend
- 3 tbsp milk
- 1 baked 9-inch deep-dish pie crust

## Instructions
1. Preheat the oven to 400°F.
2. Melt the butter in a heavy large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the mushrooms and cook until softened, stirring occasionally, about 7 minutes. Remove from the heat.
3. Mix in the spinach soufflé, sausage, cheese, eggs, and milk. Spoon into the crust.
4. Bake until a tester inserted in the center comes out clean, 30–35 minutes. Wrap the crust edges in foil if they brown too quickly.
5. Let stand at room temperature 5–10 minutes to firm before serving.
