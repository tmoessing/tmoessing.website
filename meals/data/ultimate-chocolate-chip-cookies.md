## Ingredients
- ¾ stick Crisco butter-flavor shortening
- 1 ¼ cups firmly packed light brown sugar
- 2 tbsp milk
- 1 tbsp vanilla extract
- 1 large egg
- 1 ¾ cups all-purpose flour
- 1 tsp salt
- ¾ tsp baking soda
- 1 package (6 oz) semi-sweet chocolate chips (1 cup)
- 1 cup coarsely chopped pecans (optional)

## Instructions
1. Heat the oven to 375°F.
2. Beat the shortening and brown sugar in a large bowl at medium speed until light and fluffy. Beat in the milk, vanilla, and egg.
3. Stir in the flour, salt, and baking soda. Blend in the chocolate chips and nuts.
4. Drop by rounded tablespoonfuls 2 inches apart onto ungreased cookie sheets.
5. Bake 8–10 minutes for chewy cookies or 11–13 minutes for crisp ones. Cool 2 minutes on the sheet, then move to a rack.

## Tips
- If you leave out the nuts, add another ½ cup semi-sweet chocolate chips.

## Notes
The original says "2 t milk," likely 2 tablespoons (Crisco's printed version of this recipe uses 2 tbsp).
