## Ingredients
- 1 1/2 lb chicken tenders
- 1 bunch asparagus, woody ends trimmed
- 12 oz fettuccine
- 1 jar (16 oz) Alfredo sauce
- 1/2 cup BBQ sauce
- 1 tbsp oil
- Salt and pepper to taste

## Instructions
1. Heat the grill to medium-high and start a large pot of salted water boiling.
2. Toss the chicken and asparagus with the oil, salt, and pepper.
3. Cook the fettuccine according to the package. Save 1/2 cup of the pasta water, then drain.
4. Grill the chicken 3–4 minutes per side, until done (165°F), brushing with the BBQ sauce for the last 2 minutes. Grill the asparagus 4–6 minutes, turning, until tender-crisp.
5. Warm the Alfredo sauce over low heat and toss with the fettuccine, loosening with pasta water as needed.
6. Cut the asparagus into bite-size pieces and slice the chicken. Serve over the pasta.

## Tips
- No grill? Use a grill pan or skillet over medium-high heat.
