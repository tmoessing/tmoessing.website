## Ingredients
- 1 can (16 oz) chili beans with juice
- 2 cans of water (use the chili bean can)
- 1 can (15 oz) corn with juice
- 1 can (8 oz) tomato sauce
- 2 cans (14.5 oz) diced tomatoes
- 2 cans (4 oz) chopped green chilies
- 1 package taco seasoning mix
- 2–3 thawed chicken breasts

## Instructions
1. Add everything to the crock pot and stir to mix.
2. Cook on high for 4 hours or low for 8 hours.

## Tips
- Serve with tortilla chips, cheese, and sour cream.
