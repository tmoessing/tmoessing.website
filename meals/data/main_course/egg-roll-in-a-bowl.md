## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp avocado oil
- 1 lb ground turkey (or ground beef or pork)
- 1 (16 oz) bag coleslaw mix
- 4 tbsp coconut aminos or soy sauce
- 2 tbsp sriracha, plus more to serve
- 1 tbsp sugar-free honey (or regular honey)
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp ground ginger
- 1/2 tsp chili flakes
- Pinch of salt and pepper
- Green onions and sesame seeds, to top

## Instructions
1. Heat the avocado oil in a large skillet and cook the ground turkey until golden brown. Season with the onion powder, garlic powder, ginger, chili flakes, salt, and pepper.
2. Add the coleslaw mix and let it cook down for 1–2 minutes.
3. Stir in the coconut aminos (or soy sauce), sesame oil, sriracha, and honey.
4. Top with green onions, sesame seeds, and extra sriracha if you like.

## Notes
Use coconut aminos instead of soy sauce to keep it gluten free.
