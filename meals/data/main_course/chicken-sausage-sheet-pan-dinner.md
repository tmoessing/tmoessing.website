## Ingredients
- 12–15 oz sausage links (breakfast pork links or chicken apple sausage)
- 1 sweet potato, cut into 1/2-inch cubes
- 1 bag (12 oz) frozen broccoli florets, or 1 head fresh broccoli
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- Salt and pepper to taste

## Instructions
1. Heat the oven to 425°F and line a sheet pan with parchment paper.
2. Slice the sausage into 1/2-inch rounds. Cut fresh broccoli, if using, into florets.
3. Put the sausage, sweet potato, and broccoli on the pan. Toss with the olive oil, salt, and pepper, then spread in a single layer.
4. Bake 30–40 minutes, tossing halfway through, until everything is browned and the sausage is done (160°F for raw pork).

## Notes
The original recipe uses 15 oz of fully cooked chicken apple sausage. The shopping list uses breakfast pork sausage links instead.
