## Ingredients
- 1 lb boneless, skinless chicken breast
- 3 lemons
- 3 tbsp olive oil, divided
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp fresh thyme, chopped, plus more to garnish
- 1/2 tsp kosher salt
- 1/2 tsp black pepper

## Instructions
1. Zest and juice 2 of the lemons. Warm the zest and juice in a small pot with 2 tbsp of the oil, the garlic, thyme, salt, and pepper.
2. Slice the third lemon into thin rounds.
3. Heat the last tbsp of oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Add the chicken and lemon slices and cook about 5 minutes per side, until the chicken is golden and reaches 165°F.
4. Spoon the warm sauce over the chicken and lemon slices and garnish with more thyme.
