## Ingredients
- ½ bushel ripe tomatoes (25 lbs)
- 1 head of celery, leaves and stalks
- ¼ cup salt (plain, non-iodized)
- ¾ lb butter
- 6 green peppers (4 ½ cups)
- 6 onions (4 ½ cups)
- 1 cup flour (or ¾ cup)
- 1 cup sugar (or 1 ½ cups, to taste)

## Instructions
1. Put the peppers, onions, and celery through a food chopper and cook in a small amount of water until tender.
2. Cook the tomatoes, then remove the skins and strain.
3. Add the cooked vegetables to the tomatoes.
4. Combine the flour and butter, blend with a little hot soup, then add it to the rest of the soup. Stir until well blended.
5. Add the salt and sugar. Simmer for 2–3 hours, then boil 10–12 minutes and bottle.

## Notes
Kathleen's note: simmer for 2–3 hours before placing in bottles. Makes 12 quarts.

From "Cook Like Kathleen," Grandma Kathleen Moessing's family cookbook.
