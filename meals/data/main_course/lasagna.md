## Ingredients
- 1 lb ground beef
- 3 cups spaghetti sauce
- 6 dry lasagna noodles (no need to boil)
- Cottage cheese
- 2 cups mozzarella cheese
- 1/4 cup water

## Instructions
1. Preheat the oven to 375°F.
2. Brown the beef and drain the grease. Stir in the spaghetti sauce.
3. In a rectangular dish, layer noodles, beef and sauce, then cheese. Make two layers, ending with cheese on top.
4. Slowly pour the water around the edge of the dish.
5. Cover and bake for 45 minutes. Uncover and bake 10 more minutes.
6. Let stand 10 minutes before serving.

## Notes
The water cooks the dry noodles right in the oven, so there's no boiling step.
