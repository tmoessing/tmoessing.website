## Ingredients
- 6 chicken breasts (bone-in, skin-on)
- 3 carrots, diced
- 3 stalks celery, diced
- 1 whole onion, diced
- 1 batch [Homemade Noodles](#homemade-noodles)
- Flour, for tossing the noodles

**If using boneless, skinless chicken breasts, also add:**
- 1 tsp salt
- 2 tsp chicken bouillon

## Instructions
1. Bring the chicken to a boil in a large pot of water, then simmer for 2 hours, or until the meat falls off the bone.
2. Remove the chicken. When cool, skin and debone it.
3. Dice the carrots, celery, and onion and add them to the simmering broth.
4. Add the chicken back to the pot and bring to a boil if it isn't already.
5. Toss the homemade noodles with flour and add them to the pot. Cook 5 minutes or less.

## Variations
- **Boneless, skinless breasts:** boil the breasts with the onion, celery, carrot, salt, and bouillon. When the chicken is cooked, remove and shred it while the veggies keep boiling, then return it to the pot and continue with step 5.

## Notes
From "Cook Like Kathleen," Grandma Kathleen Moessing's family cookbook.
