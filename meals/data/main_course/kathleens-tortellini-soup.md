## Ingredients
- 2 quarts chicken broth
- 2 quarts tomatoes
- 3 stalks celery
- 6 carrots, sliced
- 2 small onions, chopped
- 1 clove garlic
- 1 tbsp dried basil
- 1 ½ tbsp oregano leaves
- 2 boxes tortellini

## Instructions
1. Bring the broth and tomatoes to a boil in a large pot.
2. Add the celery, carrots, onions, garlic, basil, and oregano. Simmer until the vegetables are tender, about 20 minutes.
3. Add the tortellini and cook until tender, per the package directions.

## Notes
The original card lists only the ingredients; the steps above are the standard method for this kind of soup.
