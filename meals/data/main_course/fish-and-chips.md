## Ingredients
- ½ cup water
- 1 cup flour
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp baking powder
- White fish, like cod
- Oil, for frying

## Instructions
1. Whisk the water, flour, salt, and baking powder into a batter.
2. Pat the fish dry and cut into smaller pieces.
3. Dip in the batter.
4. Fry in oil over medium heat (you don't want it to pop) until golden brown.

## Notes
From "Cook Like Kathleen," Grandma Kathleen Moessing's family cookbook.
