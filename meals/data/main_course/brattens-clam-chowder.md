## Ingredients
- 1 cup diced onions
- 1 cup diced celery
- ¾ cup butter
- ¾ cup flour
- 1 quart half-and-half
- ½ tsp salt
- Dash pepper
- ½ tsp sugar
- 3 cans (6 ½ oz) clams
- 2 cans sliced potatoes, diced

## Instructions
1. Sauté the onions and celery in the butter until tender.
2. Add the flour. Use some of the half-and-half to make a smooth paste without lumps, then stir in the rest to thicken.
3. Add the salt, pepper, sugar, clams, and potatoes.
4. Heat gently, stirring, until hot and thick (don't let it boil hard).

## Notes
The original stops after the ingredients go in; step 4 is the usual finish.

From "Cook Like Kathleen," Grandma Kathleen Moessing's family cookbook.
