## Ingredients
- 1 container Kirkland Signature tortilla soup
- 2 cups Costco rotisserie chicken, shredded
- 1 can corn, drained (or Costco frozen street corn)
- 1 can black beans, drained and rinsed
- Grated cheese, sour cream, and tortilla chips, for serving

## Instructions
1. Heat all the ingredients on the stove.
2. Serve with grated cheese, sour cream, and tortilla chips.

## Notes
From "Cook Like Kathleen," Grandma Kathleen Moessing's family cookbook.
