## Ingredients
- ½ cup butter
- 2 tbsp fresh lemon juice
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 garlic clove, minced
- ⅛ tsp pepper
- ½ tsp paprika
- 1 can (6 oz) sliced mushrooms, drained
- 1 tbsp drained capers
- 4 chicken breasts

## Instructions
1. Put all the ingredients except the chicken in a skillet and bring to a boil.
2. Arrange the chicken in the skillet, bring back to a boil, and cover.
3. Simmer for 30 minutes, or until the chicken is tender, turning several times.

## Notes
From "Cook Like Kathleen," Grandma Kathleen Moessing's family cookbook.
