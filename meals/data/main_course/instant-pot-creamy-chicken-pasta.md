## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp oil
- 2 tsp minced garlic
- 8 oz mushrooms
- 2 chicken breasts, cut into 1/2" pieces
- 1 can chicken stock
- 1 cup milk, half & half, or cream
- 1 lb pasta
- 8 oz cream cheese
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Parmesan cheese, for serving

## Instructions
1. Turn the Instant Pot to the **Sauté** setting.
2. Cook the garlic in the oil for 1 minute, then add the mushrooms. Cook about 3 minutes, stirring now and then.
3. Add the chicken, broth, milk, and pasta. Stir.
4. Push **Cancel**.
5. Close and lock the lid, push **Pressure Cook**, and cook on high for 7 minutes.
6. Release the pressure and remove the lid.
7. Stir in the cream cheese.
8. Season with salt and pepper to taste and serve with parmesan.
