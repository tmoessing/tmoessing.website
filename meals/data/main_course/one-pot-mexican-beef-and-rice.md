## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp minced garlic
- 1 lb ground beef
- 1 tbsp taco seasoning
- 1 cup salsa
- 1/2 tbsp tomato paste
- 2 1/2 cups chicken broth
- 1 3/4 cups long grain white rice
- 1 1/3 cups canned corn, drained
- 1 cup canned black beans, drained and rinsed
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/4 cup chopped green onions
- 16 oz shredded mozzarella, divided
- Chopped fresh cilantro, to taste

## Instructions
1. Heat the oil in a Dutch oven or deep skillet over medium-high heat. Stir in the garlic for 30 seconds.
2. Add the beef and cook, breaking it up, until fully browned.
3. Mix in the taco seasoning, then the salsa and tomato paste.
4. Stir in the broth, rice, corn, beans, and salt. Cover and cook over medium-low heat 20–25 minutes, until the liquid is absorbed.
5. Stir in the green onions and half the cheese. Sprinkle the rest on top and cover 2 minutes to melt.
6. Top with cilantro and serve.
