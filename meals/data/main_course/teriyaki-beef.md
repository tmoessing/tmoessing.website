## Ingredients
- 2 lbs round steak
- 2 tbsp salad (vegetable) oil
- Water
- ⅓ cup soy sauce
- 2 tsp sugar
- ¼ tsp pepper
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 3 carrots, peeled
- 2 green peppers, cut
- ½ lb mushrooms
- 1 can (8 oz) water chestnuts
- 2 tbsp cornstarch
- ¼ cup water

## Instructions
1. Cut the steak into strips ⅛ inch thick. Brown the strips in hot oil.
2. Pour off the drippings, measure, and add water to make 1 cup. Combine with the soy sauce, sugar, pepper, and garlic and add to the meat.
3. Cover and cook slowly for 40 minutes.
4. Meanwhile, cut the carrots lengthwise into thin strips with a vegetable peeler, then cut them in half.
5. Add the carrots, peppers, mushrooms, and water chestnuts to the meat. Cover and cook 15 minutes more.
6. Combine the cornstarch and ¼ cup water and stir in to thicken the sauce.

## Notes
The original steps also mention scallions, which aren't in the ingredient list. Add a few if you have them.
