## Ingredients
**Baklava**
- 1 lb filo
- 1 lb butter (no substitutes), melted
- 1 cup sugar
- 2 tsp cinnamon
- 16 oz walnuts

**Syrup** (make before starting the baklava)
- 2 cups sugar
- 1 cup water
- ½ cup light Karo syrup
- Juice from ½ lemon (1 tbsp)

## Instructions
1. **Syrup first:** bring the sugar, water, Karo, and lemon juice to a boil. Simmer 20–25 minutes, watching so it doesn't burn. Let it cool.
2. Heat the oven to 350°F. Chop or grind the walnuts very fine and mix with the sugar and cinnamon.
3. Line the bottom of the pan with 4 filo sheets that have been buttered (2 sheets buttered and doubled over).
4. Sprinkle with the nut mixture, then repeat the filo and nut layers with as many layers as you have nuts. The top layer should be 3–5 filo sheets, buttered and doubled. Use plenty of butter.
5. Cut the baklava in diamond shapes, starting from opposite corners.
6. Bake 35 minutes at 350°F. Pour the COOL syrup over the HOT baklava.

## Notes
Kathleen spelled it "Baclava."

From "Cook Like Kathleen," Grandma Kathleen Moessing's family cookbook.
