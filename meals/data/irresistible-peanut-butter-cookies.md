## Ingredients
- ¾ cup Jif creamy peanut butter
- ½ stick Crisco all-vegetable shortening
- 1 ¼ cups firmly packed brown sugar
- 3 tbsp milk
- 1 tbsp vanilla extract
- 1 large egg
- 1 ¾ cups all-purpose flour
- ¾ tsp baking soda
- ¾ tsp salt

## Instructions
1. Heat the oven to 375°F.
2. Combine the peanut butter, shortening, brown sugar, milk, and vanilla in a large bowl. Beat at medium speed until well blended. Add the egg and beat just until blended.
3. Combine the flour, baking soda, and salt. Add to the creamed mixture at low speed and mix just until blended.
4. Drop by rounded tablespoonfuls 2 inches apart onto a greased baking sheet. Flatten slightly in a crisscross pattern with the tines of a fork.
5. Bake one sheet at a time for 7–8 minutes, until set and just beginning to brown. Cool 2 minutes on the sheet, then move to racks.

## Notes
From "Cook Like Kathleen," Grandma Kathleen Moessing's family cookbook.
