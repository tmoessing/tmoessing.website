## Ingredients
- 1 lb ground beef
- 1/2 onion, chopped
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp pepper
- 1 cup salsa
- 1 tbsp chili powder
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp cumin
- 2 cans (10 oz) enchilada sauce (red or green)
- Corn tortillas
- Shredded cheese

## Instructions
1. Preheat the oven to 350°F.
2. In a large skillet, brown the ground beef with the onion. Season with salt, pepper, chili powder, paprika, and cumin. Stir in the salsa.
3. Simmer over low heat for about 10 minutes so the flavors combine.
4. Pour a little enchilada sauce over the bottom of a 9x13 baking pan.
5. Warm the rest of the sauce in a saucepan, then dip each tortilla in it.
6. Spoon meat mixture into each tortilla, roll it up, and place seam-side down in the pan. Repeat until the meat is gone.
7. Pour the remaining sauce over the enchiladas and top with cheese.
8. Bake for 10 minutes, until heated through.
