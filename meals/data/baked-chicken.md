## Ingredients
- Chicken breasts (1 breast feeds 1–2 people)
- 1 packet McCormick Grill Mates seasoning

## Instructions
1. Preheat the oven to 450°F.
2. Season the chicken with the seasoning.
3. Line a shallow metal pan with foil (dull side up) for easier cleanup.
4. Bake 10 minutes, turn the chicken over, and bake 10 minutes more.

## Tips
- Serve with potatoes, rice, or noodles, in soft tacos, or sliced over a salad.
