## Ingredients
- 1 lb fresh green beans
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp red pepper flakes
- 1 tbsp teriyaki sauce
- Salt to taste
- ¼ cup slivered almonds

## Instructions
1. Boil the fresh beans until tender.
2. Sauté them in the olive oil with the red pepper flakes.
3. Add salt and the teriyaki sauce, and simmer until hot.
4. Add the slivered almonds and serve.

## Notes
From "Cook Like Kathleen," Grandma Kathleen Moessing's family cookbook.
