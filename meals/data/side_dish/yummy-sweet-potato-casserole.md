## Ingredients
**Sweet Potatoes**
- 4 cups peeled, cubed sweet potatoes (about 2 ½ lbs whole)
- 2 large eggs, beaten
- ½ cup sugar
- ½ cup milk
- 4 tbsp butter, softened
- ½ tsp vanilla
- ½ tsp salt

**Pecan Topping**
- ½ cup packed brown sugar
- ⅓ cup flour
- 3 tbsp butter, softened
- ½ cup chopped pecans

## Instructions
1. Heat the oven to 325°F.
2. Cover the sweet potatoes with water in a saucepan and boil until fork-tender, 10–15 minutes. Drain.
3. Mash the sweet potatoes, then mix in the eggs. Add the sugar, milk, butter, vanilla, and salt and mix until smooth. Spread in a 9x13 baking dish.
4. Mix the brown sugar and flour, then cut in the butter until it looks like coarse crumbs. Stir in the pecans and sprinkle over the sweet potatoes.
5. Bake about 30 minutes, until the topping is lightly browned.

## Tips
- Make it up to 2 days ahead: refrigerate the filling and topping separately, then top and bake (add a few minutes if it's cold).
- Leftovers keep 3–4 days. Reheat covered at 350°F for 20–25 minutes.
- Several reviewers say the filling is thin in a 9x13; use more sweet potatoes or an 8x8 pan if you want it thicker.
