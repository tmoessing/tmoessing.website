## Ingredients
- 2 yeast cakes (or 2 packets active dry yeast)
- 2 cups warm water
- 2 tsp salt
- ½ cup sugar
- 2 eggs, beaten
- ½ cup shortening
- 4–6 cups flour (for a soft dough)

## Instructions
1. Dissolve the yeast in the water and add the salt, sugar, eggs, and shortening.
2. Add the flour a little at a time, mixing well with a spoon.
3. Knead until it feels spongy. Cover and let rise until double in bulk.
4. Knead down and shape into rolls.
5. When doubled in bulk again, bake about 15 minutes at 350°F.

## Notes
From "Cook Like Kathleen," Grandma Kathleen Moessing's family cookbook.
