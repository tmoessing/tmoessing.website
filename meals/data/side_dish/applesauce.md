## Ingredients
- Apples
- About ½ cup sugar, to taste
- Water

## Instructions
1. Peel and slice the apples and add sugar to taste. Start with ½ cup; that's usually all she used.
2. Add water so the apples aren't completely covered, and watch so they don't burn. For thick sauce, don't add much water, but you need enough to cook the apples.
3. Cook until soft.
4. Pour into bottles and put on new, sterile lids and rings. Never put hot fruit in cold bottles.
5. Leave on the counter until they seal.

## Notes
These are Grandma's own instructions. For current tested canning methods, check the USDA or your local extension office.

From "Cook Like Kathleen," Grandma Kathleen Moessing's family cookbook.
