## Ingredients
- 1 ½ cups flour, sifted
- 2 tsp baking powder
- ¾ tsp salt
- ¾ cup milk

## Instructions
1. Stir the flour, baking powder, and salt together.
2. Stir in the milk only until blended.
3. Spoon the dough onto simmering chicken soup or stew to make 6–8 dumplings.
4. Simmer gently, uncovered, for 10 minutes, then cover and cook 10 minutes more. Serve.

## Tips
- Success secret: the dumplings should rest on the chicken and not settle into the liquid.
