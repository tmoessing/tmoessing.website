## Ingredients
- 1 jar (32 oz) Steinfelder sauerkraut
- 1 large yellow onion
- 6 strips bacon
- ½ tsp caraway seeds
- 1 ½ tbsp sugar

## Instructions
1. Press the fluid from the sauerkraut jar using a spoon (or your fist). Refill the jar with clear tap water and press the water out.
2. Cut the onion into quarters lengthwise, then slice thinly.
3. In a large frying pan, fry the bacon crisp and remove it from the pan.
4. Fry the onions in the remaining grease until golden brown.
5. Add the drained sauerkraut and stir slowly. When hot, add the caraway seed and sugar and simmer for 15 minutes.
6. Stir in the crumbled bacon before serving.

## Notes
Recipe from Pepi Edlinger. "We will be combining all the sauerkraut in crockpots on the serving table."

From "Cook Like Kathleen," Grandma Kathleen Moessing's family cookbook.
