## Ingredients
**Bowl 1**
- ⅓ cup warm water
- 1 tsp sugar
- 2 tbsp + 2 tsp yeast

**Bowl 2**
- 5 cups warm water
- 1 tbsp + 1 tsp salt, rounded
- 1 cup honey
- 1 cup oil
- 1 ½ cups milk
- 5 cups whole wheat flour
- 9 ⅔ cups white flour

## Instructions
1. Mix the ingredients for each bowl separately.
2. Combine the 2 bowls into one large bowl and let rise until doubled in bulk.
3. Remove from the bowl and divide into greased pans. Let rise in the pans for another hour.
4. Bake at 350°F for 30 minutes (add 5–10 minutes for large pans).

## Notes
From "Cook Like Kathleen," Grandma Kathleen Moessing's family cookbook.
