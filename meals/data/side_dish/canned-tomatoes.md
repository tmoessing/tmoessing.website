## Ingredients
- Tomatoes (Romas make the best sauce)
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp sugar

## Instructions
1. Put the tomatoes into boiling water for 15 minutes. Let them cool in the pot and water until you can peel them.
2. Peel, put in another pot, and add the salt and sugar.
3. Bring to a simmering boil for 30 minutes. For tomato sauce, cook longer until it's thick.
4. If you see seeds, smooth it with a stick blender.

## Notes
These are Grandma's own instructions. For current tested canning methods, check the USDA or your local extension office.

From "Cook Like Kathleen," Grandma Kathleen Moessing's family cookbook.
