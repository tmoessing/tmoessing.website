## Ingredients
- 1 sourdough round
- Swiss cheese slices
- 1 cup butter

## Instructions
1. Heat the oven to 375°F.
2. Cut the top of the loaf into 2-inch squares, almost all the way down.
3. Put Swiss cheese slices in each slit.
4. Melt the butter and pour it over the top so it gets into all the little crevices.
5. Bake 15 minutes, or until the cheese melts.

## Notes
From "Cook Like Kathleen," Grandma Kathleen Moessing's family cookbook.
