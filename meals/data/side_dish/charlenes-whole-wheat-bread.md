## Ingredients
**Sponge**
- ⅓ cup warm water
- 1 tsp sugar
- 2 tbsp + 2 tsp yeast, rounded

**Dough**
- 5 cups warm water
- 1 tbsp + 1 tsp salt, rounded
- 1 cup honey
- 1 cup vegetable oil
- 9 ⅔ cups white flour
- 5 cups whole wheat flour

## Instructions
1. Mix the warm water, sugar, and yeast and let it stand until foamy.
2. In a large bowl, mix the warm water, salt, honey, and oil. Add the yeast mixture, then the flours, and knead into a smooth dough.
3. Let rise until doubled, then divide into 4 greased loaf pans and let rise again, about 1 hour.
4. Bake at 350°F for 30 minutes.

## Variations
- Substitute 2 cups rye for part of the 5 cups whole wheat.
