## Ingredients
**Yeast mixture**
- 1 package yeast
- ¼ cup warm water
- 1 tsp sugar

**Dry ingredients**
- 3 ½ cups flour
- ¼ cup sugar
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 stick butter

**Liquids**
- 1 cup heavy cream (or evaporated milk)
- ¼ cup evaporated milk
- 3 egg yolks

## Instructions
1. Mix the yeast, warm water, and sugar and let stand.
2. Cut the butter into the dry ingredients as for pie crust.
3. Mix the cream, evaporated milk, and egg yolks.
4. Make a well in the center of the dry ingredients. Pour in the yeast mixture and liquids and stir until moist.
5. Put in a large bowl, spread butter or Crisco over the top, cover with plastic wrap, and refrigerate overnight.
6. Two hours before baking, take the dough out and knead to work the butter in.
7. Cut the dough in half. Roll each half into a big circle no thinner than ¼ inch and cut into about 12 wedges.
8. Roll up from the fat end to the point, like crescent rolls. Put on a parchment-lined cookie sheet.
9. Brush with melted butter, cover, and let rise 2 hours.
10. Bake at 350°F for 12 minutes.

## Notes
From "Cook Like Kathleen," Grandma Kathleen Moessing's family cookbook.
