## Ingredients
- 1 beaten egg
- 2 tbsp milk (in each half of the egg shell)
- ½ tsp salt
- About 1 cup flour (enough for a stiff dough)

## Instructions
1. In a bowl, mix the egg, milk, and salt.
2. Add flour to make a stiff dough.
3. Roll out super thin on a floured surface.
4. Cut into strips with a pizza cutter.

## Tips
- Her mother Audrey taught her to sprinkle the noodles with flour to help thicken the soup.
- Use them in Grandma's [Chicken Noodle Soup](#chicken-noodle-soup).
