## Ingredients
- 1 cup flour
- 1 cup milk
- ½ tsp salt
- 2 eggs, well beaten
- ¼ cup butter (or ¼ cup hot roast drippings)

## Instructions
1. Heat the oven to 400°F. Melt the butter in a 9x13 glass pan in the oven.
2. Sift the flour and salt together.
3. Mix the milk and eggs, add to the dry ingredients, and beat with a rotary beater until smooth.
4. Pour the batter into the hot pan and bake for 30 minutes.

## Tips
- Making a roast? Use ¼ cup hot drippings instead of the butter.

## Notes
