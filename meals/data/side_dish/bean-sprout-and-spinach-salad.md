## Ingredients
**Salad**
- 1 cup fresh bean sprouts (or 1 cup canned sprouts, drained)
- ½ lb fresh young spinach
- ½ cup thinly sliced water chestnuts

**Dressing**
- ½ cup sesame seed oil or peanut oil
- ¼ cup soy sauce
- 2 tbsp lemon juice
- 1 ½ tbsp grated onion
- 1 ½ tbsp sesame seeds, lightly toasted
- ½ tsp sugar
- ½ tsp pepper

## Instructions
1. Toast the sesame seeds in a moderate oven (350°F) for 10–12 minutes.
2. Make the dressing: combine the oil, soy sauce, lemon juice, onion, sesame seeds, sugar, and pepper. Let it stand 1 hour.
3. Pour boiling water over the fresh bean sprouts in a saucepan and heat over moderate heat without letting them boil. Drain, then soak in cold water until chilled. Drain and reserve. (Canned sprouts: soak in cold water for several hours and drain thoroughly.)
4. Wash the spinach in several changes of water. Cut off the stems, dry the leaves thoroughly, and chill.
5. Arrange the spinach in a salad bowl, top with the sprouts, then the water chestnuts. Pour the dressing over and toss thoroughly.
