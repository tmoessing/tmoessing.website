## Ingredients
- 1 lb ground beef or ground turkey
- 1 can black beans (or kidney beans)
- 1 can diced tomatoes
- 1 can tomato sauce
- 1–2 tbsp dehydrated onions (optional)
- Salt and pepper to taste

## Instructions
1. Cook the beef in a skillet over medium-high heat until browned.
2. Add the remaining ingredients and heat until bubbling.
3. Serve right away, or cover, reduce the heat, and simmer for up to 1 hour.

## Tips
- Serve in bowls, on baked potatoes, on a bed of lettuce, or over tortilla chips.
- Top with cheese, sour cream, lettuce, tomato, and more.
