## Ingredients
- 1 loaf (14 oz) French bread
- 10 oz sliced roast beef
- 8 slices Swiss cheese
- 1 packet (1 oz) au jus gravy mix, plus the water called for on the packet

## Instructions
1. Heat the oven to 400°F.
2. Make the au jus according to the packet and keep it warm.
3. Cut the bread into 4 pieces and split each one. Place cut side up on a baking sheet and toast 3–4 minutes.
4. Dip the roast beef in the au jus to warm it, then layer it on the bottom halves and top with the Swiss.
5. Bake 3–4 minutes, until the cheese melts. Add the tops and serve with the au jus for dipping.
