## Ingredients
- 2 cups uncooked rice
- 6 chicken tenders
- 1 can cream of chicken soup
- 4 cups chicken broth
- 1 package onion soup mix (or just salt and pepper)

## Instructions
1. Preheat the oven to 325°F.
2. Spray or butter the bottom of a 9x13 glass pan.
3. Spread the uncooked rice in the pan and add the broth.
4. Place the raw chicken on top and sprinkle with the soup mix (or salt and pepper).
5. Spread the cream of chicken soup over the top.
6. Bake uncovered for 1 hour, or until the broth is absorbed.
