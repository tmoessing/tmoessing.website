## Ingredients
- ¾ stick Crisco baking sticks (or ¾ cup Crisco all-vegetable shortening)
- ¾ cup sugar
- ¾ cup firmly packed brown sugar
- 2 large eggs
- 2 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1 tsp ground cinnamon
- ¼ tsp salt
- 2 ¾ cups rolled oats
- 1 cup chopped walnuts

## Instructions
1. Heat the oven to 375°F. Lightly coat baking sheets with cooking spray.
2. Beat the shortening, sugar, and brown sugar in a large bowl at medium speed until creamy. Beat in the eggs and vanilla.
3. Combine the flour, baking soda, cinnamon, and salt in a small bowl. Beat into the shortening mixture until blended.
4. Stir in the oats and walnuts.
5. Drop by rounded tablespoonfuls about 2 inches apart on the baking sheets.
6. Bake 10–12 minutes, or until light brown. Cool on the sheets 2 minutes, then move to a rack to cool completely.

## Notes
From "Cook Like Kathleen," Grandma Kathleen Moessing's family cookbook.
