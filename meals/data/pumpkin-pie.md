## Ingredients
- 3 cups mashed pumpkin (1 large can)
- 1 ½ cups milk (1 cup evaporated milk and ½ cup whole milk)
- 1 ¾ cups sugar
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 ½ tsp ginger
- 1 tbsp cinnamon
- 4 tbsp molasses
- 3 eggs
- 1 tsp lemon juice
- 1 unbaked pie shell

## Instructions
1. Heat the oven to 425°F.
2. Mix all the filling ingredients together until smooth.
3. Pour into the unbaked pie shell.
4. Bake at 425°F for 15 minutes, then at 350°F for 35–50 minutes more.
5. Serve with whipped cream.

## Notes
From "Cook Like Kathleen," Grandma Kathleen Moessing's family cookbook.
