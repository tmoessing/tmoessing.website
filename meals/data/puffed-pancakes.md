## Ingredients
- 1/4 to 1/3 cup salted butter
- 3–4 eggs
- 3/4 to 1 cup milk
- 3/4 to 1 cup flour

## Instructions
1. Put the butter in the baking pan and set it in a 425°F oven. Mix the batter while the butter melts.
2. Blend the eggs on high speed for 1 minute.
3. With the motor running, gradually pour in the milk, then slowly add the flour.
4. Keep blending for 30 more seconds.
5. Pour the batter into the hot melted butter. Bake until puffy and brown, 20–25 minutes.

## Notes
Use the smaller amounts for a smaller pan and the larger amounts for a bigger one.
