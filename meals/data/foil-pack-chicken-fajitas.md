## Ingredients
- 1 1/2 cups rice
- 1 1/2 cups hot water
- 1 tbsp taco seasoning
- 4 boneless chicken breast halves (about 1 lb)
- 1 green and 1 red bell pepper, sliced
- 1/2 cup salsa
- 1/2 cup shredded cheese

## Instructions
1. Heat the oven to 400°F.
2. Fold up all sides of 4 large sheets of heavy-duty foil to form a 1-inch rim, and spray with cooking spray.
3. Combine the rice, hot water, and taco seasoning and spoon evenly onto the foil. Top with the remaining ingredients.
4. Bring up the foil sides and fold to make 4 packets. Place on a rimmed baking sheet.
5. Bake 30–35 minutes, until the chicken is done (165°F). Cool 5 minutes, then cut slits in the foil to release steam before carefully opening.

## Tips
- Make the packets ahead and refrigerate up to 1 day, then bake as directed.
- Add the liquids last so the packets are easier to fold.
- Go heavier on the taco seasoning.
