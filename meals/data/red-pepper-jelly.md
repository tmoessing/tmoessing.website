## Ingredients
- 1 ½ lb red bell peppers (about 3), cut into 1-inch pieces (6 cups)
- 1 tsp dried hot red-pepper flakes
- 3 tbsp Sure-Jell less- or no-sugar-needed pectin (from a 1 ¾ oz box)
- 3 ¼ cups sugar, divided
- 1 cup white-wine vinegar
- 1 tbsp unsalted butter
- ¾ tsp salt

## Instructions
1. Pulse the bell peppers and pepper flakes in a food processor until finely chopped.
2. Whisk the pectin with ¼ cup of the sugar in a small bowl.
3. Stir together the pepper mixture, vinegar, butter, salt, and the remaining 3 cups sugar in a 5–6 qt heavy pot.
4. Bring to a vigorous boil over high heat and keep boiling vigorously, stirring occasionally, for 5 minutes.
5. Gradually add the pectin mixture, whisking constantly. Return to a vigorous boil, stirring constantly, and boil 1–2 minutes (it will thicken slightly).
6. Remove from the heat.
