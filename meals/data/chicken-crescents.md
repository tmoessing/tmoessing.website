## Ingredients
- 1 cup cooked, shredded chicken (drained canned chicken works great)
- 1 package (8 oz) cream cheese
- 2 tbsp butter
- 1 can (8 oz) Pillsbury crescent rolls

## Instructions
1. Preheat the oven according to the crescent roll package.
2. Mix together the chicken, cream cheese, and butter.
3. Open the can and separate the crescents. Spread each one open into a triangle (easier if the dough sits out of the fridge for a few minutes).
4. Put a spoonful or more of the chicken mixture in the center of each roll.
5. Roll the long, pointed end over the filling toward the other two ends, then fold the 2 ends toward each other.
6. Bake as directed on the crescent package.

## Variations
- Dip them in melted butter, then roll in 1/2 cup crushed Saltines or Ritz crackers before baking.
