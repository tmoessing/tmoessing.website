## Ingredients
- 1 bag frozen mixed vegetables, thawed
- 1 cup cut-up cooked chicken
- 1 can (10.75 oz) condensed cream of chicken soup
- 1 cup Original Bisquick mix
- 1/2 cup milk
- 1 egg

## Instructions
1. Preheat the oven to 400°F.
2. In an ungreased 9-inch glass pie plate, stir together the vegetables, chicken, and soup.
3. In a small bowl, stir the Bisquick, milk, and egg with a fork until blended. Pour over the chicken mixture.
4. Bake about 30 minutes, until golden brown.
