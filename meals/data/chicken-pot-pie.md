## Ingredients
- 1 bag frozen mixed vegetables, thawed
- 1 cup cut-up cooked chicken
- 1 can (10.75 oz) condensed cream of chicken soup
- 1 cup Original Bisquick mix
- 1/2 cup milk
- 1 egg

## Instructions
1. Preheat the oven to 400°F.
2. In an ungreased 9-inch glass pie plate, stir together the vegetables, chicken, and soup.
3. In a small bowl, stir the Bisquick, milk, and egg with a fork until blended. Pour over the chicken mixture.
4. Bake about 30 minutes, until golden brown.

## Variations
- **Big batch (9x13), ingredients:** 2–3 cans cooked chicken, 2 cans cream of chicken soup, 1 package frozen vegetables, 2 cups Bisquick, and 1 1/2 cups milk (no egg).
- **Big batch, directions:** Mix the chicken, soup, and vegetables in an ungreased 9x13 glass dish. Stir the Bisquick and milk until blended and pour over the top. Bake at 400°F for 40–45 minutes, until a knife comes out clean and the top is a little golden. For the cart, add 1 or 2 more cans of chicken and 1 more can of soup.
- **Freeze half:** Split the big batch between two 8x8 baking dishes and freeze one for later.
