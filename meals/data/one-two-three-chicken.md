## Ingredients
- 4 small chicken breasts (or 6–8 chicken tenders)
- 1 jar (16 oz) salsa
- 1 can black beans
- 1 cup shredded cheese

## Instructions
1. Cook the chicken in a non-stick skillet over medium-high heat, 4 minutes per side.
2. Add the salsa and beans. Bring to a boil and cover.
3. Simmer on medium-low for 5 minutes, or until the chicken reaches 165°F.
4. Top with the cheese and remove from the heat. Let stand, covered, until the cheese melts.

## Tips
- Serve over potatoes, rice, or chips.
