## Ingredients
- 1 corned beef roast
- Sauerkraut, boiled potatoes, and carrots, for serving

## Instructions
1. Boil the corned beef in water according to the package directions.
2. Remove the fat.
3. Serve with sauerkraut, boiled potatoes, and carrots.

## Tips
- Grandma's [Sauerkraut](#sauerkraut) goes great with it.

## Notes
From "Cook Like Kathleen," Grandma Kathleen Moessing's family cookbook.
